## Patterns observed across deals

**Lease-up at acquisition** (CocoMar, Rialto, Southeast Austin, Unbound) — A&T vacancy + free rent in Y1. Modeled today via `MarketLeasingAssumption.downtime_months_new` + `Lease.free_rent_months`. Works for single-tenant deals (Unbound).

**Mid-hold rollover events** (Telephone Road, partial Rialto / Southeast Austin) — A&T vacancy and TI/LC bills appear in mid-hold years when a tenant rolls. Not yet modeled — needs multi-tenant lease support (one Lease per tenant) and the projector to emit TI/LC for MLA-spawned segments. Phase B+ schema work.

**Year-varying Vacancy Allowance / Credit Loss** (Telephone Road shows constant % of SBR in stable years but spikes during rollover years). Today `Property.general_vacancy_pct` is a single fraction. Phase B option: extend to per-year `dict[int, Decimal]` or auto-compute during transitional years.

**Property Management Fee** is percentage-of-revenue (varies year-to-year). Already supported via `opex_with_pct_of_revenue_fee` helper + `Property.opex_categories`.

## Schema follow-ups to close remaining gaps

1. **Multi-tenant rent rolls + speculative rollover TI/LC.** Today the projector emits TI/LC only at original lease commencement. MLA-spawned new-tenant segments should bill TI/LC on their commencement too. Unblocks the leasing-cost rows in Telephone Road, Rialto, Southeast Austin.
2. **Per-year vacancy / credit loss overrides.** Either `Property.general_vacancy_by_year: dict[int, Decimal]` or computed from rollover detection. Unblocks Vacancy Allowance / Credit Loss matching in Telephone Road.
3. **`Property.capex_categories` for Capital Reserves** is shipped — but Telephone Road's reserves of $58K growing 3%/yr aren't in any stub yet. Mechanical to add once a stub is built.
4. **Y11 partial-year handling.** Every deal's Y11 is mid-year-anchored (period starts month ≠ Jan for some deals). Projection truncation in the engine already handles this; reporter output just needs a label that flags partial years.
